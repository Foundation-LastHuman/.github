## About

LASTHUMAN Foundation (Fundacja LastHuman) is an independent global think tank based in Warsaw, Poland, operating internationally to protect and advance the role of humanity in the age of artificial intelligence. We work to ensure that technology strengthens human agency, dignity and freedom.

The Foundation operates under the Polish Act on Foundations of 6 April 1984 and is registered in the National Court Register under KRS 0001228457. Its statute is public: [Foundation Statute (PDF)](https://lasthuman.today/assets/statut-lasthuman-2026.pdf). No member of the Management Board receives remuneration for serving on the Foundation's governing bodies, nor for creating texts, graphics, designs, films, educational materials or other works for the Foundation.

Our work is open by default. The tools, research and educational resources we produce are meant to stay accessible to practitioners, researchers, policymakers and the public alike.

## Mission

Our mission is to build a future where AI and advanced technologies are developed *for* humans, *with* humans and *around* humans. Technology should serve humanity, never replace it.

## 2030 vision

- AI aligned with human values
- Inclusive access to technological progress
- Strong democratic governance of emerging technologies
- Human creativity amplified, not displaced

## Programs

The Foundation runs six programs: three core programs and three community programs. Each program has four priorities.

### Core programs

#### [Responsible AI & Ethics](https://lasthuman.today/responsible-ai)
Shaping ethical frameworks and guidelines for AI development that prioritize human values and societal benefit.
**Priorities:** Bias · Transparency · Accountability · Governance

#### [AI Safety Research](https://lasthuman.today/ai-safety)
Investigating AI alignment, robustness and safety mechanisms to ensure artificial intelligence remains beneficial and controllable.
**Priorities:** Research · Publications · Partners · Experts

#### [Technology Policy](https://lasthuman.today/tech-policy)
Influencing international technology policy through research, advocacy and collaboration with policymakers worldwide.
**Priorities:** AI Regulation · Digital Rights · Data Governance · International Cooperation

### Community programs

#### [Education & Digital Transformation](https://lasthuman.today/digital-transformation)
Supporting individuals and communities navigating digital transformation with education, resources and guidance.
**Priorities:** Businesses · Schools · NGOs · Citizens

#### [Social Impact & Digital Inclusion](https://lasthuman.today/social-impact)
Developing programs that address the digital divide and algorithmic bias, and ensure equitable access to the benefits of AI across all communities.
**Priorities:** Digital Divide · AI Accessibility · Algorithmic Fairness · Community Empowerment

#### [Human-Centered Technology Design](https://lasthuman.today/human-design)
Designing systems and solutions that put people first, ensuring technology serves human needs and aspirations.
**Priorities:** Human-Centered AI Systems · Ethical AI Experiences · User Trust and AI Adoption · Inclusive Design

## How we work

- Conducting and funding research on AI safety, technology ethics and AI's impact on society
- Organizing conferences, seminars, workshops, training, exhibitions and hackathons
- Publishing reports, analyses, academic articles, books, podcasts and educational materials
- Collaborating with academic institutions, universities, NGOs, public bodies and businesses in Poland and abroad
- Developing recommendations, standards and guidelines for the ethical development and use of AI
- Advocating for responsible technology policy
- Running social programs that counter digital exclusion and algorithmic bias
- Building online platforms, information portals, applications and knowledge bases
- Initiating and joining national and international projects, consortia and partnerships
- Creating mentoring and acceleration programs for innovators working on responsible AI
- Providing consulting and advisory services on AI ethics and responsible deployment
